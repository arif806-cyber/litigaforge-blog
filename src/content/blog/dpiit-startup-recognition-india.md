# India Startup Recognition 2026

The Indian government has introduced the DPIIT startup recognition program to promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the country, providing numerous benefits to recognized startups. To take advantage of these benefits, it's essential to understand the eligibility criteria and step-by-step application process for DPIIT startup recognition in India.

## Benefits of DPIIT Startup Recognition

The DPIIT startup recognition program offers various benefits to recognized startups, including tax exemptions under Section 80-IAC of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and exemption from certain provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, such as Section 25F, which deals with retrenchment. Additionally, recognized startups are eligible for funding from the government's Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) and can access various government schemes, such as the Startup India initiative. The recognition also provides a boost to the startup's credibility and reputation, making it more attractive to investors and customers. The process of recognition is governed by the Startup India initiative, which was launched in 2016 to promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the country.

> **Key takeaway:** Startups recognized by DPIIT can access tax exemptions, funding, and other benefits to promote their growth and development.

## Eligibility Criteria for DPIIT Startup Recognition

To be eligible for DPIIT startup recognition, a startup must meet certain criteria, including being incorporated as a private limited company or a registered partnership firm, having a turnover of less than Rs. 100 crores, and being less than 10 years old. The startup must also be working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products or services, and must have a scalable business model with a high potential for employment generation or wealth creation. The eligibility criteria are outlined in the Startup India initiative and are governed by the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Partnership Act, 1932.

> **Key takeaway:** Startups must meet specific eligibility criteria, including age, turnover, and incorporation, to be recognized by DPIIT.

## Step-by-Step Application Process for DPIIT Startup Recognition

The application process for DPIIT startup recognition involves several steps, including registering on the Startup India website, filling out the application form, and uploading required documents, such as the certificate of incorporation, proof of concept, and detailed business plan. The application is then reviewed by the DPIIT, and if approved, the startup is recognized and issued a recognition certificate.
