## The Current Legal Framework: §218 StGB and its Exceptions

This counseling is crucial; it must be conducted by a state-recognized counseling center, aiming to support the woman in her decision and offer assistance, but without influencing her choice. The counselor issues a certificate (Beratungsschein) confirming the counseling, which is then presented to the performing physician. A further exception exists for pregnancies resulting from rape or other sexual offenses, where an abortion is permitted up to fourteen weeks after conception without mandatory counseling, though counseling is usually still offered.
