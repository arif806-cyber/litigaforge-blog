The UK redundancy law is governed by the Employment Rights Act 1996, which provides the framework for handling redundancies. The consultation period must last for at least 30 days for 20-99 redundancies and 45 days for 100 or more redundancies. The selection criteria for redundancy must be fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory.
