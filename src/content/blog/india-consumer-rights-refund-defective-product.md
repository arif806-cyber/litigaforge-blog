# India Consumer Rights 2026

When an e-commerce company in India refuses to refund a defective product, consumers can take action under the Consumer Protection Act 2019. In this article, we will guide you through the process of seeking a refund and the relevant laws that protect your consumer rights in India.

## Understanding Consumer Rights in India

The Consumer Protection Act 2019 is the primary law that governs consumer rights in India. According to Section 2(7) of the Act, a consumer is defined as any person who buys goods or services for a consideration. The Act provides for the establishment of consumer councils and forums to promote and protect consumer rights. Under Section 20 of the Act, a consumer can file a complaint with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission for any deficiency in service or defect in goods. The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 also provide guidelines for e-commerce companies to ensure consumer protection.

> **Key takeaway:** Consumers in India have the right to seek a refund or replacement for defective products under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

If an e-commerce company refuses to refund a defective product, the consumer can take the following steps: 1. Send a notice to the company, 2. File a complaint with the consumer forum, 3. The complaint should include details of the product, the defect, and the refusal of the company to refund.

> **Key takeaway:** Consumers should send a notice to the e-commerce company before filing a complaint with the consumer forum.
