## CPF Contribution Rates 2026

The CPF contribution rates for 2026 are governed by the Central Provident Fund Act (Cap 36), which outlines the requirements for both employers and employees. As of 2026, the employer contribution rate is 17% of the employee's salary, while the employee contribution rate is 20% of their salary, as per the CPF Act Section 40. It is essential to note that these rates apply to Singaporean citizens and permanent residents, while foreign workers are exempt from making CPF contributions, as stated in the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act (Cap 91A) Section 14.
