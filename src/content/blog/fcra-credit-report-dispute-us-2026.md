## Understanding the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)

The FCRA is a federal law that regulates the collection, use, and disclosure of consumer credit information. Under Section 609 of the FCRA, consumers have the right to dispute inaccurate or incomplete information on their credit reports. The law requires credit reporting agencies (CRAs) to investigate disputes within 30 days and correct or delete any inaccurate information.
