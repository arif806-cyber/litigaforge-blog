# UAE Golden Visa 2026

The UAE golden visa is a long-term residence visa that offers a wide range of benefits to its holders, including 100% ownership of businesses and no requirement for a sponsor. To be eligible for the UAE golden visa in 2026, applicants must meet specific criteria and submit the required documents as per the UAE's Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2018.

## Eligibility Criteria for UAE Golden Visa 2026

The UAE golden visa is available to various categories of individuals, including investors, entrepreneurs, scientists, and specialized talents. According to Article 3 of the Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2018, the eligibility criteria for the UAE golden visa include: 1. Investors: A minimum investment of AED 2 million in a UAE-based company or a real estate property. 2. Entrepreneurs: A minimum capital of AED 500,000 for a startup business. 3. Scientists: A PhD degree in a relevant field and a significant contribution to the scientific community. 4. Specialized Talents: A bachelor's degree or higher in a relevant field and a minimum of 5 years of experience. The UAE golden visa is granted for a period of 5 or 10 years, as per Article 5 of the Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2018. The visa can be renewed upon expiration, subject to meeting the eligibility criteria.
