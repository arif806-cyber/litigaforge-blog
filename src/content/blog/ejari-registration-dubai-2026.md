## What is Ejari Registration?

Ejari registration is a mandatory process in Dubai, governed by Law No. 26 of 2007, which requires all tenancy agreements to be registered with the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA). To register with Ejari, tenants and landlords must provide the required documents, including a copy of the tenancy agreement, a copy of the tenant's passport, and a copy of the landlord's Emirates ID.
