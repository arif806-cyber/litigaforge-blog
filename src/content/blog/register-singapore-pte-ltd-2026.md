# How to Register a Singapore Pte Ltd in 2026 – Step-by-Step Guide

Starting a company in Singapore in 2026 is fast, transparent, and cost‑effective. This guide walks you through the exact ACRA steps, statutory requirements and fees to get your Pte Ltd up and running.

## 1. Legal Framework Governing Singapore Private Limited Companies

The incorporation of a private limited company (Pte Ltd) in Singapore is governed primarily by the Companies Act (Cap. 50). Key provisions include Section 13 (Name of company), Section 14 (Business activities), Section 15 (Registered office), Section 17 (Share capital), and Section 45 (Filing of annual returns). The Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) enforces compliance under the Act and its subsidiary legislation, such as the Companies (Incorporation) Regulations 2023. Failure to comply with Section 45 can result in a fine of up to SGD 10,000 per default or imprisonment for up to six months under Section 333 of the Act. Understanding these statutes ensures your company meets statutory obligations from day one.

> **Key takeaway:** Know the core sections of the Companies Act to avoid penalties during incorporation.

## 2. Choosing a Company Name and Reserving It with ACRA

Step 1 is to select a unique name that complies with the Naming Guidelines (Section 13, Companies Act). The name must not be identical or confusingly similar to existing entities, must not be offensive, and cannot suggest a connection with a government body. Using ACRA’s online portal BizFile+, you submit a Name Reservation Request (NRR). The reservation fee is SGD 15, and the name is held for 60 days. If the name is rejected, ACRA provides a reason under Section 13(2) and you may amend and resubmit within 14 days. For foreign founders, ensure the name does not breach the Foreign Companies Act (Cap. 332).

> **Key takeaway:** Reserve your company name on BizFile+ and address any ACRA objections within 14 days.

## 3. Preparing Incorporation Documents and Filing with BizFile+

The core documents required are: (a) Constitution (formerly Articles of Association) – must comply with Section 15 of the Companies Act; (b) Signed consent to act as director (Form 45) – each director must be at least 18 years old and not disqualified under Section 152; (c) Shareholder resolution appointing the first directors and approving the share allotment; and (d) Registered office address proof. All documents are uploaded to BizFile+ in PDF format. The filing fee for a standard Pte Ltd is SGD 315 (including the registration fee of SGD 300 and the name reservation fee). Upon successful filing, ACRA issues an electronic Certificate of Incorporation within minutes, confirming the company’s registration number (UEN).

> **Key takeaway:** Upload the Constitution, director consents, and share details on BizFile+ to receive your UEN instantly.

## 4. Post‑Incorporation Compliance: Licenses, Taxes and Statutory Registers

After incorporation, you must: (i) Open a corporate bank account – most banks require the UEN, the Constitution, and a board resolution; (ii) Register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) if annual turnover exceeds SGD 1 million, as per the GST Act (Cap. 117), Section 9; (iii) File the first Annual Return within 30 days of the Annual General Meeting (AGM) under Section 45 of the Companies Act; (iv) Maintain statutory registers (share register, register of directors, register of charges) as required by Section 176; and (v) Appoint a qualified Company Secretary within 6 months of incorporation under Section 165. Non‑compliance with the Company Secretary requirement can attract a fine of up to SGD 5,000 per day under Section 333. For foreign-owned Pte Ltds, a work pass for any expatriate director must be obtained under the Employment Pass framework, governed by the Employment Pass Regulations 2024.

> **Key takeaway:** Set up a bank account, GST registration and appoint a Company Secretary within six months to stay compliant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to register a Singapore Pte Ltd?

Typically 7‑10 business days from name reservation to receiving the Certificate of Incorporation, provided all documents are complete.

### What is the minimum share capital required?

Singapore does not impose a minimum; most founders start with SGD 1, but a higher amount may be needed for certain licences.

### Do I need a local director or shareholder?

At least one director must be a Singapore resident (citizen, PR, or holder of an Employment Pass). Shareholders can be foreign individuals or entities.

### Can I use a standard template constitution?

Yes, ACRA provides a Model Constitution, but you may customise it as long as it complies with Section 15 of the Companies Act.
