# TUPE Transfer UK 2026

The Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 (TUPE) safeguard employees' rights when a business changes hands in the UK. ## What is TUPE Transfer UK?

The Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 (TUPE) is a UK law that protects employees' rights when a business is transferred from one owner to another. This includes mergers, acquisitions, and outsourcing. TUPE applies to both public and private sector organizations. According to Section 3 of TUPE, the transferor (the old employer) must provide the transferee (the new employer) with employee liability information, including details of employment contracts, collective agreements, and any pending employment tribunal claims. The Employment Rights Act 1996 also plays a crucial role in protecting employees' rights during a TUPE transfer. Employers must comply with the Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations 2004, which requires them to inform and consult with employees about the transfer. In the case of Manchester College v Hazel (2015), the Employment Appeal Tribunal (EAT) ruled that TUPE applies to all types of business transfers, including those involving public sector organizations.

> **Key takeaway:** Employers must provide employee liability information to the new employer and inform and consult with employees about the transfer.

During a TUPE transfer, employees have several rights that are protected by law.
