# UK Graduate Visa 2026

If you're an international student in the UK, switching from a student visa to a graduate visa can be a great way to extend your stay and gain work experience. ## Eligibility Criteria for Graduate Visa UK 2026

To be eligible for a graduate visa, you must have completed a UK degree, such as a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD, and have a valid student visa. The graduate visa route is open to international students who have completed their studies in the UK and want to work or look for work in the UK. As per the Immigration Rules 2022, you must also meet the requirements of Appendix Graduate, which includes having a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your university and meeting the English language requirement. You can apply for a graduate visa within 3 months of completing your course, but you must apply before your student visa expires. The UK Visa and Immigration department will consider factors like your academic qualifications, language proficiency, and maintenance funds when assessing your application.

> **Key takeaway:** You must apply for a graduate visa within 3 months of completing your course.

With a graduate visa, you will have the right to work in the UK, including self-employment and voluntary work.
