## What is a Prenuptial Agreement?

A prenuptial agreement, also known as a prenup, is a contract between two individuals planning to get married. This agreement typically outlines the property and financial rights of each partner in the event of a divorce or the death of one spouse. Under the Uniform Premarital Agreement Act (UPAA), which has been adopted by most states in the US, prenuptial agreements are recognized and enforced.
