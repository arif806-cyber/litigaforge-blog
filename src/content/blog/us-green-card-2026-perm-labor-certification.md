## What is PERM Labor Certification?

PERM labor certification is a process under the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA) Section 212(a)(5)(A) that allows US employers to sponsor foreign workers for permanent residence. The process involves the employer obtaining a labor certification from the US Department of Labor (DOL) to demonstrate that there are no qualified US workers available for the job. The employer must also show that hiring the foreign worker will not adversely affect the wages and working conditions of similarly employed US workers.
