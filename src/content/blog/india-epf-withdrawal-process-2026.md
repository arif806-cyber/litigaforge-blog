# India EPF Withdrawal 2026

With the introduction of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, Indian employees can now easily withdraw their EPF online and offline, but the process can be complex. In this article, we will guide you through the EPF withdrawal process in India 2026, including the necessary documents, timelines, and penalties for non-compliance, as per the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 Section 25F.

## Eligibility Criteria for EPF Withdrawal

To withdraw your EPF in India, you must meet the eligibility criteria set by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). According to the EPF Scheme 1952, an employee can withdraw their EPF under certain circumstances, such as retirement, resignation, or death. The employee must have completed a minimum of 10 years of service to be eligible for a full EPF withdrawal. However, if the employee has not completed 10 years of service, they can still withdraw their EPF, but the amount will be proportionate to their service period. The EPFO has also introduced the Universal Account Number (UAN), which allows employees to manage their EPF accounts easily. As per the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, an employee is eligible for gratuity if they have completed 5 years of continuous service.

> **Key takeaway:** Check your eligibility criteria before initiating the EPF withdrawal process to avoid rejection.

## Documents Required for EPF Withdrawal

To initiate the EPF withdrawal process, you will need to submit certain documents to the EPFO. The required documents include the EPF withdrawal form, Aadhaar card, PAN card, and bank account details. The employee must also provide a cancelled cheque or bank account passbook to facilitate the transfer of the EPF amount. The EPFO has introduced the online submission of documents, which has simplified the process. However, if the employee is not able to submit the documents online, they can submit them offline at the nearest EPFO office. As per the Income Tax Act 1961 Section 80C, the EPF withdrawal amount is taxable if the employee has not completed 5 years of continuous service.

> **Key takeaway:** Gather all the necessary documents before initiating the EPF withdrawal process to avoid delays.

## Online EPF Withdrawal Process

The online EPF withdrawal process is a convenient and efficient way to initiate the withdrawal of your EPF. To start the process, log in to the UAN portal and click on the 'Withdrawal' option. Fill in the required details, such as the reason for withdrawal, and upload the necessary documents. The EPFO will verify the documents and process the withdrawal amount. The online EPF withdrawal process is faster and more secure than the offline process. As per the Information Technology Act 2000, the EPFO is responsible for ensuring the security and confidentiality of the online transactions.

> **Key takeaway:** Use the online EPF withdrawal process to initiate the withdrawal of your EPF quickly and securely.

## Offline EPF Withdrawal Process

The offline EPF withdrawal process involves submitting the EPF withdrawal form and supporting documents to the nearest EPFO office. The employee must fill in the form carefully and attach the required documents, such as the Aadhaar card and PAN card. The EPFO will verify the documents and process the withdrawal amount. The offline EPF withdrawal process is more time-consuming than the online process, but it is still a viable option for employees who do not have access to the internet. As per the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 Section 10, the EPFO is responsible for ensuring the timely and efficient processing of the EPF withdrawal applications.

> **Key takeaway:** Use the offline EPF withdrawal process if you do not have access to the internet or prefer to submit the documents in person.
