# UAE Contracts 2026

In the UAE, employees and employers often debate the merits of limited vs unlimited contracts, with termination rules being a key consideration. ## Limited Contracts in UAE

Limited contracts in the UAE are employment contracts with a fixed term, typically ranging from one to three years, as per the UAE Labor Law No. 8 of 1980, Article 38. These contracts are subject to renewal or termination upon expiration. The key characteristic of limited contracts is that they are time-bound, and employers are required to provide employees with a minimum notice period of 30 days, as stated in Article 113 of the UAE Labor Law. In the event of termination, the employer must pay the employee any accrued but unpaid salary, as well as any entitlements due, such as annual leave and gratuity, as per Article 53 of the UAE Labor Law. It's essential to note that limited contracts can be terminated by either party, provided the required notice period is given, and the termination is not arbitrary or discriminatory, as per Article 122 of the UAE Labor Law.

> **Key takeaway:** Employers must provide a minimum notice period of 30 days when terminating a limited contract in the UAE.

## Unlimited Contracts in UAE

Unlimited contracts, on the other hand, are employment contracts with no fixed term, as per Article 38 of the UAE Labor Law. These contracts continue indefinitely until terminated by either party, provided the required notice period is given. The notice period for unlimited contracts is also 30 days, as stated in Article 117 of the UAE Labor Law.
