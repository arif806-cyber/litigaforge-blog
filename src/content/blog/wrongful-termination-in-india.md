# India Wrongful Termination Notice 2026

If you've been wrongfully terminated from your job in India, sending a legal notice to your employer is the first step towards seeking justice. ## Understanding Wrongful Termination in India

Wrongful termination, also known as unfair dismissal, occurs when an employer terminates an employee's contract without a valid reason or without following the proper procedure. In India, the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, provides protection to employees against wrongful termination. According to this section, an employer must provide a valid reason for termination and follow the proper procedure, which includes providing the employee with a notice period of at least 30 days. The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, also provides for penalties for employers who wrongfully terminate employees, including reinstatement and compensation. For instance, in the case of Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd. vs. S. Ramachandra, the Supreme Court of India held that an employee who is wrongfully terminated is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.

> **Key takeaway:** Employees in India have the right to seek justice against wrongful termination under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

## Grounds for Sending a Legal Notice for Wrongful Termination

Before sending a legal notice, it's essential to understand the grounds for wrongful termination. These grounds include termination without a valid reason, termination without following the proper procedure, and termination based on discriminatory grounds such as caste, religion, or gender. The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, and the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, also provide protection to employees against wrongful termination. For example, if an employer terminates an employee without providing the required notice period or without paying the employee's gratuity, the employee can send a legal notice to the employer. The notice should specify the grounds for wrongful termination and the relief sought, which can include reinstatement, compensation, or damages.

> **Key takeaway:** Employees can send a legal notice to their employer for wrongful termination based on various grounds, including termination without a valid reason or proper procedure.
