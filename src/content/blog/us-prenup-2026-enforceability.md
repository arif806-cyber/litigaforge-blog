## What is a Prenuptial Agreement?

A prenuptial agreement, also known as a prenup, is a contract between two individuals planning to get married, outlining the division of assets, debts, and other financial responsibilities in the event of a divorce. The UPAA defines a premarital agreement as 'an agreement between prospective spouses made in contemplation of marriage and to be effective upon marriage' (Section 1, UPAA). In the US, the enforceability of a prenup is determined by the state in which the agreement was signed, with some states like California, Florida, and New York having specific laws governing prenuptial agreements. ## How Courts Review Prenuptial Agreements
