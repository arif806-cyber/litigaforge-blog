In India, tenant rights are governed by the Rent Control Act of 1948, which varies from state to state. ## Security Deposit Refund Process

The security deposit refund process in India is governed by the terms of the tenancy agreement and the applicable rent control laws. Under Section 17 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, a landlord is required to refund the security deposit to the tenant within one month of the termination of the tenancy. If the landlord fails to refund the deposit, the tenant can approach the court for relief.
