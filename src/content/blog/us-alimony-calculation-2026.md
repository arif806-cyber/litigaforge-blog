## Types of Alimony

There are several types of alimony that can be awarded in the US, including temporary alimony, rehabilitative alimony, and permanent alimony. Temporary alimony, also known as pendente lite alimony, is awarded during the divorce proceedings to support the lower-earning spouse. Rehabilitative alimony, on the other hand, is designed to help the supported spouse become self-sufficient, as seen in the UAE's Federal Law No. 28 of 2005, Article 59, which emphasizes the importance of rehabilitation in alimony awards. Permanent alimony, also known as alimony pendente lite, is typically awarded in long-term marriages where one spouse has limited earning capacity.
