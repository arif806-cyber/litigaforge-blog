## Understanding Prenuptial Agreements

A prenuptial agreement, also known as a premarital agreement, is a contract between two individuals planning to get married, outlining the division of assets, debts, and other financial responsibilities in the event of a divorce. The Uniform Premarital Agreement Act (UPAA) of 1983, adopted by most states, provides guidelines for the creation and enforcement of prenuptial agreements. Under Section 6 of the UPAA, a prenuptial agreement is not enforceable if the party against whom enforcement is sought proves that they did not execute the agreement voluntarily or that the agreement was unconscionable at the time of execution.
