# Singapore HDB Subletting 2026

If you're a homeowner in Singapore, understanding the HDB subletting rules is crucial to avoid penalties and ensure a smooth rental process. ## Eligibility to Sublet

To sublet your HDB flat, you must meet the eligibility criteria set out by the Housing and Development Board (HDB). According to the HDB's rules, you can sublet your flat if you have fulfilled the Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) of 5 years, or 3 years for flats bought from the open market. Additionally, you must obtain approval from HDB before subletting your flat, as required under Section 24 of the Housing and Development Act (Cap 129). You can sublet your flat to either Singaporeans, Permanent Residents, or non-citizens, but you must ensure that the total number of occupants does not exceed the maximum allowed, which is 6 people for a 3-room or smaller flat, and 9 people for a 4-room or larger flat. > **Key takeaway:** You must obtain approval from HDB before subletting your flat, and ensure that the total number of occupants does not exceed the maximum allowed.
