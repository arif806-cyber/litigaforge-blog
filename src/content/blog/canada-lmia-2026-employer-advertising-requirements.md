## Understanding LMIA and its Purpose

The Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) is a process used by Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) to assess the impact of hiring a temporary foreign worker on the Canadian labour market. As per Section 203 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (IRPR), an LMIA is required for most temporary foreign worker applications. The purpose of an LMIA is to ensure that the hiring of a temporary foreign worker will not have a negative impact on the Canadian labour market and that there are no qualified Canadian workers available to fill the position.
