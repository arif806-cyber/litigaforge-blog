## Limited Contracts in UAE

Limited contracts in the UAE are governed by Federal Law No. 8 of 1980, also known as the Labour Law. According to Article 38 of this law, limited contracts are those that have a specific duration, typically ranging from one to three years. These contracts can be renewed, but the renewal must be in writing and agreed upon by both parties. If a limited contract is terminated before its expiration date, the terminating party may be liable for damages, as per Article 116 of the Labour Law. For instance, if an employer terminates a limited contract without notice, they may be required to pay the employee compensation equivalent to three months' salary, as stated in Article 117. It's essential to note that limited contracts can provide a level of job security for employees, as employers must have a valid reason for termination, as outlined in Article 120 of the Labour Law.

## Unlimited Contracts in UAE

Unlimited contracts, on the other hand, are also governed by Federal Law No. 8 of 1980, but they do not have a specific duration. ## Termination Rules for Limited and Unlimited Contracts

Both limited and unlimited contracts in the UAE have specific termination rules. For unlimited contracts, termination can occur if one party gives the other party notice, or if the employee resigns, as per Article 121 of the Labour Law. In both cases, the terminating party must provide the other party with written notice, stating the reason for termination, as outlined in Article 122 of the Labour Law.
